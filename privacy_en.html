Squeezly · Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 8, 2026

Squeezly ("we", "the app") respects user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data is processed when you use the Squeezly mobile application on Apple iOS or Google Android.

1. Data We Collect

1.1 Files you provide

The files you select for compression — including PDF documents, images, and videos — are processed only on your device. These files are:

  • Never uploaded to any server.
  • Never viewed by us or any third party.
  • Stored only at the location you choose, after compression.

1.2 Automatically collected data (advertising)

The free tier shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may automatically collect:

  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android) — only when you grant permission (see Section 1.4).
  • Coarse IP address — used by AdMob to limit ad serving and prevent fraud.
  • Device type, OS version, app version, language — for ad performance analytics.
  • Crash logs and performance data related to ad rendering.
  • Ad interactions (impressions, clicks).

Squeezly itself does not store, log, or transmit this data. Google's processing is governed by Google's own privacy policy (linked in Section 2).

1.3 In-app counters stored on your device

  • Daily compression count, total bytes saved, and similar statistics — kept only on your device and never transmitted.
  • Premium entitlement status — stored locally and synchronized with the App Store / Google Play receipt for the signed-in account.

1.4 App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS 14.5 and later, when you first launch Squeezly we display the standard Apple App Tracking Transparency prompt before initialising any advertising SDK. You may choose:

  • Allow — AdMob may use your IDFA to deliver personalised ads. Ads remain shown either way.
  • Ask App Not to Track — AdMob serves only contextual (non-personalised) ads; the IDFA is not used.

You can change your decision at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

1.5 What we do NOT collect

  • Your real name, email, or phone number (unless you contact support).
  • Your precise location (no GPS access requested).
  • Your contacts, calendar, microphone, or health data.
  • The content of files you compress.
  • No account registration is required — Squeezly does not maintain user accounts.

3. Required Reasons API (iOS Privacy Manifest)

Squeezly's iOS Privacy Manifest declares the following Apple "Required Reasons" API categories:

  • FileTimestamp (C617.1) — to read file modification times when compressing.
  • SystemBootTime (35F9.1) — to measure compression duration.
  • DiskSpace (E174.1) — to write the compressed output and temporary files.
  • UserDefaults (CA92.1) — to persist your local settings and premium entitlement.

None of these APIs are used to track you across apps or websites.

4. Data Retention

  • Files you compressed: kept on your device until you delete them.
  • Local statistics: kept on your device, removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Premium entitlement: managed by Apple App Store or Google Play; subscription history is held by the respective store.

5. Children's Privacy

Squeezly is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from this age group.

6. Your Rights

Under GDPR (EU), KVKK (Turkey) and similar laws you have the rights below:

  • Access: All your data is visible in the app's "Settings" section.
  • Deletion: Uninstalling the app deletes all local data. To cancel a subscription, use Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on iOS, or your Google Play account on Android.
  • Ad personalisation: On iOS, change tracking permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, reset your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.

7. Security

Because your files are not uploaded, network interception is not possible. Premium payments are processed by Apple's or Google's encrypted billing infrastructure — we never see your card details.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced via in-app notification.
