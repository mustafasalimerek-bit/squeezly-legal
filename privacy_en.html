Squeezly · Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

Squeezly ("we", "the app") is built to respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Squeezly mobile application handles your data.

Squeezly works 100% on your device. It contains no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and requires no account. Your files and personal content never leave your phone.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on any server. Squeezly has no backend.

1.1 Files you compress

PDFs, images, videos, GIFs, ZIP archives, Office documents and other files you select are processed only on your device:

  • Never sent to any server.
  • Never seen by us or any third party.
  • Saved only where you choose, after compression.

1.2 Cleanup scans (photos, contacts, calendar)

To help you free up space, Squeezly can scan your photo library, contacts and calendar entirely on your device. This content is analyzed locally to suggest items to clean up; it is never uploaded or shared, and nothing is deleted without your confirmation.

1.3 On-device app data

In-app counters, such as daily compression/cleanup usage and total space saved, are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

1.4 What we do NOT do

  • No ads, no ad SDK, and no ad-related tracking.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs.
  • No account, no login, no identifying information.
  • No location, no contact upload, no file upload.

2. Third-Party Services

ServicePurposePrivacy Policy
Apple App Store / Google Play Billing Premium purchase processing only Apple · Google

Premium purchases are handled by Apple's or Google's in-app purchase systems. We never see your payment details. No other third-party SDKs are integrated.

3. Data Retention

  • Your files: Stay on your device until you delete them.
  • Usage data: On your device only; removed when you uninstall.
  • Premium status: Managed by Apple/Google; subscription history handled by the store.

4. Children's Privacy

Squeezly is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from this age group. Squeezly does not collect personal data from anyone.

5. Your Rights

Under GDPR, KVKK (Turkey) and similar laws:

  • Access: All app data lives on your device, visible in Settings.
  • Deletion: Uninstalling the app removes all local data. Manage or cancel subscriptions via your App Store / Google Play account.

6. Security

Because your files and personal content never leave your device, there is no server-side data that could be intercepted. Premium payments are encrypted by Apple/Google.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated in-app.
